On this page

Styles ​

Three styles, after three games. Each sounding character (letter, digit or other symbol) plays one sound; spaces and punctuation are silences.

Beep ​

One beep for every letter, like Undertale. The same short square-wave beep for every character. With variation at 0 every beep has the same pitch.

  • Sound identifier: variant 0.

Grunt ​

A few grunts for each character, like Banjo-Kazooie. The voice has four grunts, different in pitch, length and timbre; each character plays one of them, chosen pseudo-randomly but deterministically, and never the same grunt twice in a row.

  • Sound identifier: variant 0–3, the grunt that plays.

Alphabet ​

A sound for every letter of the alphabet, like Animal Crossing. Each letter is a short syllable: an optional consonant attack (filtered noise) followed by a vowel made with three formants, at almost the same pitch for every letter (within ±1 semitone), so letters differ by timbre, not by note.

  • The letters a e i o u sound as the Italian vowels.
  • Consonants use the vowel of their English name (b → "bi", f → "e", q → "u").
  • Accented letters fold to their base letter (è → e, ñ → n).
  • Digits and other symbols share one neutral sound.
  • Sound identifier: variant 0–25 for a–z, 26 for the neutral sound.
